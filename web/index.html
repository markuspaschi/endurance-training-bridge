CONNECT

Connect Garmin

1Save your private key

Generated automatically and saved in this browser. Copy it now—you will paste it into the pairing helper.

Enter the same key on your phone to easily authenticate!

2Run the pairing helper

Open Terminal, paste the command below, and press Enter. It downloads the readable helper from GitHub, runs it in a disposable Python environment, and removes that environment afterward.

When asked, paste your private key, then enter your Garmin email, password, and MFA code. Credentials go directly from the helper to Garmin—not through this website.

3Finish connecting

Wait for “Pairing complete” in Terminal, then return here. Your athlete ID is detected automatically.
